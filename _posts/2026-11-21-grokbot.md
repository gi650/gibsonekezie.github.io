**How to build a self-improving grok bot**

Grok bot is an autonomous AI system designed for ease and simplicity. I say this because by design you do not require technical expertise of any kind to create one.

When we talk about “self-improving”, the first question that comes to mind is how does self-improvement occur? The idea of self-improvement is very common to humans mainly because that’s all we have known & done throughout our lives. It’s simply after performing a task and having knowledge of what went wrong, you can make changes to either avoid the previous errors or overcome them on the next task. I mean you can relate it to writing a shell script or any script basically only after running the first time, then you know what changes to make. This is similar to grok bots they require feedback to self-improve.

Some key points to consider before creating one

- What goal do you want the bot to achieve.
- How do you evaluate completion of said goal.
- How do you provide feedback.

For everyone who has used an AI chatbot or attempted creating an agent before you know the best approach is to be clear and explicit when providing your context and instructions. In that sense, grok bots are no different. One thing to be conscious of is grok bots are built to be proactive for example; the first thing the bot does once it is created is to predict its function or “goal” using its name, chat history and whatever information it has access to. Before providing instructions always ensure, you’ve thought through the steps and tools needed to achieve the goal. That’s there only way you can provide clear step by step instructions or create a skill needed by the bot to perform a task or accomplish a goal.

When it comes to how to evaluate the task and how to provide feedback. This is where you’re default bot comes in. I personally, feel like a lot of users under utilize their default bot. Your default bot can evaluate the task performed by the bot you created, grade the task and provide recommendations to help the bot self-improve.

Now let’s work through a simple demo task of creating a grok bot that finds AI fellowships programs on twitter.

When creating a new bot, you are provided with an option to name the bot and choose a profile icon. You can name the bot whatever you want, mine is named @ffinder.

Next step is to edit your bot’s profile to add a description and a title (optional).

Title: Research

Description: search X and find tweets on all fellowship posting related to AI, AI safety, AI Evaluation, AI Agent, AI Governance and AI research.

Next step: Send instructions to your bot;

1\. search for tweets containing fellowships related to these AI areas: AI, AI safety, AI Evaluation, AI Agent, AI Governance and AI research.

2\. Create a batch of your findings into a list

3\. Forward batch to @controller (my default bot)

4\. make this a weekly routine and run every Monday by 1pm

5\. Do not duplicate findings.

Final step: Send these instructions to your default bot;

1\. Inspect steps taken by @ffinder on AI fellowship research task.

2\. Answer each evaluation question with Yes, No or Not sure.

3\. Is the task completed?

4\. Is each of the programs still accepting applications?

5\. are the links provided the programs valid?

6\. Evaluate and Score @ffinder based on these questions over 100.

7\. Make recommendations to @ffinder on how to make improvements.

8\. Notify me once the task is completed.

That’s it !! you’ve successfully created a self-improving grok bot
